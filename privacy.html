Health.local privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

The short version. Health.local keeps your health data on your iPhone or iPad. It has no account, no server and no analytics, and it doesn't track you. Health.local's developer can't see your data.

What Health.local reads

With your permission, Health.local reads data from Apple Health, such as steps, active energy, heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep, weight and workouts. If you turn on Women's Health, it also reads your cycle records. You choose what to allow, and you can change that in the Health app at any time.

What Health.local writes

It saves back to Apple Health only what you log in Health.local: period days, spotting and weights.

What stays in Health.local

Symptoms you log, your Journal entries and your day tags stay in Health.local. Symptoms and Journal entries are encrypted with a key that never leaves your device.

Where your data lives

On your device only. Health.local stores its own copy in a protected, encrypted database, which is excluded from device backups. Health.local doesn't send health data anywhere.

What leaves your device

Nothing, unless you export it. When you choose Export, Health.local makes a file and you decide where it goes (Files, AirDrop, Mail or anywhere else you share to). After that, the file is yours and outside Health.local's control.

What Health.local doesn't do

It doesn't create accounts, sell or share data, show ads, use analytics or tracking, or include third-party tracking code.

Beta testing with TestFlight

If you test Health.local through Apple's TestFlight, Apple collects crash reports and basic usage information about the test app and shares them with the developer, as described in Apple's TestFlight & Privacy notice. If you send feedback, any note or screenshot you include is shared too, so a screenshot may show health numbers that were on screen. The developer uses this only to fix and improve Health.local, and you can ask for it to be deleted at the address below. Health.local itself still sends nothing.

Face ID

If you turn on the Women's Health lock, Health.local asks iOS to check your Face ID or passcode. Health.local never receives your biometric data.

Deleting your data

Settings has a Delete all data option that erases everything Health.local has stored, including symptoms and Journal entries. It doesn't delete anything in Apple Health. Deleting the app also removes its stored data.

Not medical advice

Health.local shows what Apple Health recorded and how it compares with your own usual. It doesn't diagnose or treat anything. Talk with your doctor about your health.

Children

Health.local isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new date appears above, and the app's What's New notes say so.
